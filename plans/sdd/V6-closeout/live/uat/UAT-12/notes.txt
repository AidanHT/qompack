 F-UAT12-1 (product defect; row fail criterion) runtime.mcp.maxResponseBytes bounds the content
   span, not the response: expand and re_read with full:true on the 324,902 B capture return
   263,559 / 263,567 B of result text (266,291 / 266,318 B JSON-RPC lines) against a 262,144 B bound.
   The overshoot is JSON escaping plus the envelope and grows with escape-heavy content (the blob
   capture: 2,134 content bytes became 2,948 text bytes, +38%).
 F-UAT12-2 (product vs docs) expand and re_read responses carry no fidelity and no coverage field
   (sessionB stream, cli/20); user-guide.md and the row's step 3-4 expectation say fidelity and

   arrived as host-decoded text (3,000 B file -> 2,134 B stored, U+FFFD and control characters);
