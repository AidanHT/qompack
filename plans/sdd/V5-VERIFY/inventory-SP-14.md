### 3.2 Retired

**I-14.1 (partial) and the `checkpoint-now` half of I-14.11 — "`checkpoint` and `checkpoint-now` both dispatch."**
`qompack checkpoint` is the **PreCompact hook** entry point and nothing else. `internal/cli/qompack_commands_test.go` `TestSlashCommands_AreDiscoverable` special-cases it — `require.True(t, got.Hook, "checkpoint is still the PreCompact hook entry point (H3)")` — and `TestSlashCommands_CheckpointRemainsTheHook` pins that it resolves to exactly one entry. The `/qompack:checkpoint` frontend has **no `qompack <name>` route at all**: `spec.go:82` `unroutedSubcommands` records it as needing "the `arch/checkpoint-now-subcommand` architecture pre-step (SP-14 handoff edge H3)", and `TestGenCommandDocs_DoesNotAdvertiseAnUnroutedCommand` stops the docs claiming otherwise.
*Replacement pointer:* SP-14 §Implementation spec — "Preserve the `arch/checkpoint-now-subcommand` pre-step as a future architecture dependency before any changed CLI routing"; open handoff edge **H3**.

**I-14.8 — daemon-side status op.**
No status op was added on the daemon side. `ipc.OpStatus` is the pre-existing SP-05 handler and SP-14 renders it from the client, by design: `statuscollect.go:20` states the mirror exists *because* `internal/commands` does not import `internal/daemon`, so that boundary stays visible.
*Replacement:* `StatusSchema = 1` pinned by `TestStatus_ReportSchemaIsVersioned`; mirror currency by `TestStatus_DaemonPayloadMirrorIsCurrent` plus the member-by-member copy in `internal/cli.fetchDaemonStatus`, which makes a stale mirror a **compile** error as well as a test failure; original payload preserved by `TestStatus_OriginalOpStatusIsPreserved`. Panic containment is the daemon's (`internal/daemon/handlers.go:264` and seven sibling `recover()` sites).
